# Privacy notice

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Find Me (find.appts.uk) lets you share your location with people you choose,
for a limited time. It is built so that we hold as little about you as
possible.

## There are no accounts

You do not sign in. We never ask for your name, email address, or phone number
to use the service. We do not set advertising or analytics cookies, and we do
not track you across other websites.

## What we hold, and for how long

When you create a pin we store:

- A random identifier for the pin (the code in the link).
- A one-way hash of the secret that lets you update it.
- Your message, if you wrote one.
- When the pin was created and when it expires.

Your **location** is stored separately, in an isolated store dedicated to that
single pin. It is never written to our main database, and it is deleted
automatically when the pin expires.

Everything above is deleted when the pin expires, at the latest. The longest a
pin can last is 7 days. There is no archive and no backup that outlives it.

## Who can see your location

Anyone holding your pin's public link. That is the point of the service — but
it means **you decide who sees your location by deciding who you send the link
to**. If you post that link somewhere public, your location is public.

Pins are not listed, searchable, or browsable. There is no map of other
people's pins, and the only way to find a pin is to be given its link.

## When your location is captured

Location comes from your device, and only when you ask for it — either by
placing the pin yourself or by requesting an update from your control page.
Your browser will ask permission first.

Your location updates only while the page is open. If you close the tab or
lock your phone, updating stops. We cannot track you in the background.

## Email

If you choose to have your private link emailed to you, we send that email
once and do not store your address. Our email provider (Cloudflare) keeps
delivery records — whether the message was delivered, bounced or suppressed —
under its own retention policy, which we do not control.

We only ever send this one email. There is no mailing list and no resend.

## Abuse prevention

To stop the service being used for spam or automated abuse, we store a
**hashed** version of your IP address, combined with a secret that changes
daily. This lets us count requests without being able to identify you or link
your activity across days. We do not store raw IP addresses.

## On your device

Your browser stores your pin's secret link locally so you can return to it.
This never leaves your device: clearing your browser data removes it, and we
cannot recover it for you.

## Who processes your data

The service runs entirely on Cloudflare's infrastructure (compute, database,
storage and email delivery). Map data comes from OpenStreetMap via Protomaps,
and map tiles are served from our own storage — the map provider does not see
where you are looking.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights over personal data we hold about
you, including access, correction and erasure.

In practice, the design gives you most of this directly: you can delete your
pin at any time using the stop control on your private link, and everything
expires automatically regardless. Because we hold no account and no identifier
that links a pin to you, we usually cannot locate data about you from a name
or email address alone — if you want to make a request, please include the pin
link.

## Being tracked against your will

If you believe this service is being used to track or harass you, contact
**abuse@appts.uk**. Please include the link if you have it.

If you are in immediate danger, contact the police on 999.

## Contact

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office
(ico.org.uk).
